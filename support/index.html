Tare — Support

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Tare is a personal finance app for iPhone. You scan a receipt or type a purchase in, and it lands in your ledger, categorised, with monthly totals, a breakdown by category and a list of the charges that come round regularly. Everything it does runs on your own device. There is no account to create, and nothing you enter is sent to us.

Getting help

Email Tareque Rishad at tareque.rishad@gmail.com. Say which iPhone you are using and which version of Tare (Settings shows the version at the bottom). If something went wrong, Settings › Diagnostics › Export Diagnostics… produces a file that describes what the app did; attaching it helps, and you can read it first — it is a plain text file. In Full detail it includes amounts and merchant names, in Redacted detail it does not.

Exporting your data

Settings › Export as CSV writes every transaction in your ledger to a spreadsheet file — its date, merchant, amount, currency, category, account, how you paid, tags and note, and where it came from — and lets you save or share it. It asks you to confirm it is you first. The file is yours to keep or to open in any spreadsheet.

Deleting your data

  • One transaction: swipe left on it in Transactions, or select several and delete them together. A deleted transaction disappears from every screen and from the export at once. It is kept, hidden, on your devices and in your iCloud until you use Delete all data.
  • Everything: Settings › Delete all data erases your whole ledger on this device and, if it syncs through your iCloud, on your other devices too. It also removes every receipt photograph kept on this device. It asks you to confirm it is you first, and it cannot be undone.
  • The app itself: deleting Tare from a device removes everything it kept there, including receipt photographs.

Frequently asked questions

Does Tare need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

How does my ledger reach my other devices? Through your own iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud on both devices, your transactions sync between them in your private iCloud database, which we cannot see. If you are not signed in to iCloud, Tare works on that one device and nothing syncs. Signing out of iCloud on a device, or turning iCloud off for Tare in the device's settings, removes Tare's synced copy of your ledger from that device — export as CSV first if you are unsure.

What is the lock, and how do I turn it off? Tare locks your ledger with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, and it is on by default. It first asks once you have a transaction, and it locks again about a minute after you leave the app. To turn it off, go to Settings › Privacy and switch off Require Face ID (or Touch ID); Tare asks you to confirm it is you before it does.

Where do my receipt photographs go? The picture you keep with a scanned receipt stays on the device that scanned it, in Tare's own storage. It is not sent to us and it does not sync to your other devices, which show the transaction without its picture. Like everything an app keeps, it is part of that device's backup if you make one.

Can Tare find receipts I have already photographed? Yes, if you turn it on: Settings › Find receipts in my photos. It asks for access to your photo library and then looks through new photos each time you open Tare, listing the ones that look like receipts for you to check. It is off by default, photos are read on the device, and nothing joins your ledger until you confirm it. Without it, Add › Choose Photos… lets you pick a photo yourself and needs no permission.

Can I bring in transactions from another app? Settings › Import from a file reads a CSV export from the Cost Track app. It shows you what it will add — every account, the date range and the totals per currency — before it writes anything, and Undo last import takes the whole import back.

Can I change my currency? Yes. Tare starts with the currency of your iPhone's region, and Settings › Currency changes it. Your totals, Insights and new entries then use the currency you chose. Nothing is converted: every transaction stays in the currency it was entered in, and amounts in other currencies are shown beside the totals rather than added into them. You can change it back at any time. Receipts still waiting in the list found from your photos are read again in the new currency, and each asks to be checked before it is added.

Why does a receipt read wrongly? Receipts are read on the device, and a faded, crumpled or unusual slip can confuse it. Every field is shown for you to check before anything is saved, and you can correct any transaction later by tapping it. If a receipt reads wrongly every time, tell us what it printed and what Tare read.

Is there a paid version? Not at the moment. Everything in Tare is free, and nothing in it is sent to us or costs us anything to run.
