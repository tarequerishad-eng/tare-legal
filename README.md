# Tare — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 September 2026**

Tare is a personal finance app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy says what
happens to your information, in plain words. Where the answer is "nothing leaves
your device", it says so, because for most of what Tare does that is the truth.

## The short version

- **If you do not connect a bank, Tare sends nothing to us. Ever.** There is no
  account to create, nothing to log in to, and no server involved.
- **We do not track you, and we do not collect analytics.** No advertising
  identifiers, no third-party analytics SDKs, no profiling.
- **We never sell your information**, and we never share it for advertising.
- **We never see your bank username or password.** Nobody at Tare can, by design.

## What Tare does on your device

Reading receipts, recognising text, categorising a purchase, totalling a month,
detecting a recurring charge and writing advice all run **on your iPhone, iPad or
Mac**. None of it involves a network request.

- **Receipt photographs** are read on the device. The photograph you keep with a
  receipt stays on that device and is not uploaded or synced.
- **Finding receipts in your photo library** is optional and off by default. When
  you turn it on, Tare looks at photos on that device only. It records which
  photos it has already examined — identifiers and a status, never anything it
  read from a photo.
- **Card numbers are redacted before anything is stored.** Tare keeps at most the
  last four digits, and only where the payment method can carry them.

## Sync through your own iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your ledger syncs between your own devices using
Apple's CloudKit, in **your** private iCloud database. We have no access to it —
it is your Apple account, not ours, and Apple bills the storage to you.

Your personal details in that database are **end-to-end encrypted**, so they are
unreadable even to Apple: merchant names, notes, tags, the last four digits of a
card, transfer counterparties, receipt text and addresses, account names, account
numbers' last digits, institution names, category names and rule values. Amounts,
dates, identifiers and flags are not encrypted, because CloudKit must be able to
index them for sync to work at all.

## If you connect a bank (a paid feature)

Connecting a bank is optional, is part of a paid subscription, and is the only
part of Tare that involves our servers.

- **Your bank credentials never reach us.** The connection is made through
  **Plaid**, whose own screen collects them; they travel from your device to
  Plaid and never through Tare. We receive a token that lets us read
  transactions, and nothing that could let anyone sign in to your bank.
- **We request read access to transactions only.** Not the ability to move money.
- **What we store**, encrypted, under a key unique to you: the access token, your
  account names and types, and the transactions we read, so they can be matched
  against receipts you have already entered and not counted twice.
- **You can disconnect at any time**, in the app. Disconnecting revokes the token
  with Plaid and deletes the connection.
- Plaid has its own privacy policy, at plaid.com/legal.

## If you use the optional advice feature (a paid feature)

Where Tare offers written advice generated by a language model, it sends a
summary of the figures it is describing — amounts, categories, dates — and never
your receipt images. This is a paid feature and is not used on the free tier.

## What we keep, and for how long

- Free tier: nothing, because nothing reaches us.
- Paid tier: what is listed above, for as long as you keep the subscription. When
  you delete your account, we delete it. Deleting all data in the app also erases
  what is on your device and, through your own iCloud, on your other devices.
- Every privileged action on our servers is written to an append-only audit log,
  which records that something happened and to which account — not the contents.

## Children

Tare is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their
information.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask what we hold, ask for a copy, ask for it to be
corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. If you never connected a bank, the answer
to the first question is "nothing". Export of your own ledger is built into the
app, as CSV, and needs no request.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what happens to your information, the
date at the top changes and the app will tell you before the change takes effect.
